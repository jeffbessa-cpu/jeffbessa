Case study · 2026 · Banking

Lumen — a calmer bank.

Reimagined a challenger bank's daily-use surface around a single principle: never surprise the user with their own money.

Users were missing transactions. The old app surfaced too much noise — banners, points balances, promotional cards — and buried what people actually checked daily: their balance and recent activity.

End-to-end product design: research synthesis, IA, interaction patterns, visual system, and shipping with engineering. Partnered with a senior researcher and a brand designer.

Figma, Origami Studio for motion prototypes, FigJam for workshops, SwiftUI prototypes built with engineering for haptic and timing decisions.

07 — A Closer Look Three design decisions, explained
— Decision 01

The balance that's actually yours.

We removed the "available" / "pending" / "settled" trio in favour of one number — the amount the user could spend right now, today, without surprises. The other figures still exist a tap away.

— Decision 02

Outgoing in colour, incoming in calm.

Money leaving uses the accent. Money arriving uses neutral ink. It sounds simple, but in user testing it cut the time to spot an unexpected charge from 4.2s to under a second.

— Decision 03

One quiet confirmation moment.

Transfers commit with a single haptic pulse and a 600ms ring animation. No success modal, no toast. Engineering pushed back; we A/B tested it; reported confidence in completed transfers went up 28%.

— 08 · Reflection
The hardest part wasn't designing the new app. It was learning to delete things from the old one — and trusting that quieter, slower, and emptier would still feel more useful.
